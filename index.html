GridAI: smart microgrid analytics

A web app for analysing and optimising smart microgrids. It covers 16 years (2008–2024) of electricity demand, renewable generation and battery storage data across Nigeria, Australia, Germany and Canada, matched to ERA5 climate data for each region.

Inside, it compares 48-hour solar, wind and demand forecasts from Random Forest, Gradient Boosting and XGBoost models, optimises battery charging with linear programming, and flags anomalies with an ensemble. It runs in English and French, and Sign in with Apple, Google or Microsoft protects the production app.
